    Quick Answers

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to common questions about joining Nexus Computing Society, executive recruitment, certificates, and event policies.

    General membership opens at the start of every Fall and Spring semester during Orientation Week. You can register via the Membership page or visit our desk at the Student Activities Center (Block A). Once verified with your FAST-NUCES roll number, you receive access to our Discord channels, weekly study jams, and priority workshop seats.

    Yes! Freshmen are encouraged to apply for Assistant Lead and Member Coordinator positions across our Web Engineering, AI/ML, Cybersecurity, UI/UX, and Event Operations wings. While Executive Director titles typically require at least one semester of active society contribution, 1st-semester students frequently join core technical teams based on enthusiasm and a brief portfolio or problem-solving interview.

    Verifiable digital certificates (PDF with a unique verification code) are emailed to your registered @isb.nu.edu.pk address within 5 to 7 working days after an event concludes. Attendance must be marked at check-in, and multi-day bootcamps require at least 80% session attendance or final project submission.

    Weekly study jams, coding clinics, and peer mentoring sessions are completely free for all FAST-NUCES students. Flagship hackathons and certified bootcamps carry a subsidized fee (with a 25%–50% discount for registered NCS members). Fees are 100% refundable if requested at least 48 hours before the event, or transferable to another student upon emailing our desk.

    Select Event Sponsorship / Collaboration in the inquiry form above or email nexus.society@isb.nu.edu.pk with your organization profile, proposed timeline, and collaboration format (tech talk, hackathon track sponsor, or recruitment booth). Our Corporate Relations team responds with our sponsorship prospectus within 2 business days.

    General queries and membership requests are answered within 24–48 working hours (Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM). Urgent technical issues affecting active hackathon portals, Git mirrors, or lab access are prioritized by our Infrastructure Wing the same day. Keep your generated TKT-XXXX reference ready when following up.
